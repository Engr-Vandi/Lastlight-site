Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

In short: Last Light has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your game progress stays on your phone. We do not collect your personal information.

Last Light ("the app") is a game made by Victor Vandi (victorvandi.com). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

2. Your game progress

Your progress, settings and in-game money are saved only on your phone. We cannot see them.

  • If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your progress is deleted.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your own Google backup. That is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

3. No internet connection needed

Everything the game needs, including its fonts, is inside the app. It does not connect to the internet and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

4. Permissions

The app asks for one permission: vibration, used for game feedback. You can turn it off in the game.

5. In-game money

The naira you earn in the game is not real money. It cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn, and the app has no in-app purchases.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email engr.vandi@gmail.com.

Keep the house alive for a whole year.

Mama wants the AC. Brother wants the TV. Cousin's phone is dying, Mr. Okon needs his iron, and the power has gone off again. You have one battery and a few solar panels. Make it to dawn.

Every watt is a decision

Each day runs from sunrise to dawn. The sun fills the battery, the family drains it, and you decide who gets power and who has to wait.

⚡

Share the power

Tap appliances on and off. Go over the inverter's limit and it trips. Run the battery flat and the whole house goes dark.

🏠

Keep the family happy

Everyone has a routine and a temper. Bargain, say yes, say not now, and watch their patience rise and fall.

📅

Live a whole year

365 days of dusty dry season, rainy season, heatwaves, fuel shortages, power strikes, Christmas and New Year's Eve.

🎯

Daily goals

Three new goals every day. Finish all three to build a streak and earn bonus money.

💰

Build your future

Save up to own your home, buy the Volta electric car, and turn the house into a smart home with more panels.

🌅

The light tells the time

Morning sun, golden afternoon, orange sunset, a long dark night. Plan ahead before the solar stops.
